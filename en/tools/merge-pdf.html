How it works

  1. Upload two or more PDF files.
  2. Reorder them into the sequence you want.
  3. Click Merge PDF.
  4. Download your combined document.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?⌄

There's no server limit. Practical limits depend on your device's memory and the total file size.

Are my PDFs uploaded?⌄

No. Merging is done locally with pdf-lib. Your documents never leave your device.

Will merging change the quality of my PDFs?⌄

No. Pages are copied as-is into the new document, so text, images, and resolution are preserved exactly.
